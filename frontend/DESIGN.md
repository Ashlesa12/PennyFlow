## Motion

Duration

200–300ms

No bouncy animations.

No flashy effects.

Everything should feel smooth.

---

## Layout

Large whitespace.

Balanced spacing.

Clear hierarchy.

Desktop-first.

Responsive.

---

## UX

Never use browser alerts.

Always use elegant inline feedback.

Loading should use skeletons or subtle spinners.

Empty states should be encouraging.

---

## Rules

Do not introduce new colors.

Do not redesign existing components unnecessarily.

Maintain visual consistency.

Preserve the PennyFlow design language.
